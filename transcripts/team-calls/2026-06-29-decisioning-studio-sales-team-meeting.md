## Summary

### Nathaniel's AI Taxonomy Collateral
- New deck: a tech taxonomy framework for Braze AI products, built with GenAI, currently in socialization.
- Buckets: Enriching Data, Predictive Suite, Personalized Content, Conversational AI, Experimentation, Decisioning, Workflow Agents.
- Feedback: make point-solution vs. global-solution distinction explicit (Rachael); pricing/access indicators per feature would help (Ryan); Nathaniel to hold off on pricing callouts to distribute faster.

### AI Resource Hub
- Grassroots product-marketing effort: filterable hub of all Braze AI resources (customer stories, slides, one-pagers, blogs, Looms, Seismic links), filterable by content type/industry/product.
- Erica flagged need for a strong, single, ideally industry-specific DS one-pager to help CSMs/EDRs open DS conversations.

### Wins and Pipeline
- **Zooplus** closed (procurement friction, not AI review; Spark agency helpful).
- **Green King** RFP turnaround after a low initial scoring-matrix rank; reframed use cases, proposed agent-console hackathon.
- **Purina** first use case live with strong results; textbook DS motion.
- Pipeline (Steve): Kimberly Clark, Superhuman, Exact Sciences, Shake Shack.
- **Deezer** (Louis, Paris) final stage, competing vs. High Touch on price.

### Endgame Signals Report
- Weekly report flags potential DS opportunities from AE/CSM conversations; broken out by region this week. PJ to post each Monday in the team channel.

### Multi-Workspace Limitation
- DS limited to a single workspace; friction for multi-workspace customers (Bally's, Schibsted). Workarounds + Daniel Feis sizing the problem. Flag blocker deals in Slack; don't surface the limitation too early.

### Sharing Claude Skills Across Teams
- Product marketing and AI SolCon should share Claude skills (positioning checker, blog reviewer). Move from ad hoc grassroots to deliberate cross-team sharing.
